### New Features

- **Reworked Windows auto-update** — the old flow downloaded the update inside the running app, then quit and re-ran the installer via a hidden PowerShell one-liner; a real subset of failures left you staring at a closed app with no relaunch. Auto-update now spawns a small detached helper (its own window and tray icon) that takes over once the app quits: it waits for the app to fully exit, downloads the release with visible progress, verifies its checksum, runs the installer elevated and silent, then relaunches Zentro. Every path through the helper ends in a relaunch — even a failed install won't strand you without the app — and Zentro reports whether the update succeeded the next time it starts.

### Fixes & Polish

- The update modal and toolbar update button were simplified to match the new single-step flow: the modal's call-to-action now reads "Restart & Install" on Windows and triggers the restart directly, and the toolbar button dropped its now-obsolete downloading/restart-staged states.
